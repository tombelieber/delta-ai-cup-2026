Your learning, with clear boundaries.

Delta privacy notice · Updated 21 October 2026

Delta helps you learn from YouTube content, practise and return to source-linked material. This notice covers the Delta website and Chrome extension.

Local records do not mean all processing is local. Viewing records and your saved learning library stay in this browser profile. When you request AI help, selected content is processed by Delta's backend and Google's Gemini API. Google sign-in and an account credit ledger use Firebase.

What stays in your browser

The extension stores YouTube video links, titles, available creator information, observed playback ranges, optional learning goals, imported history, topic groupings and learning results in Chrome local storage. Playback recording is enabled by default; you can turn it off in Watching records & settings. Recording a range does not establish understanding.

YouTube account-history import requires you to confirm the visible account. Optional Chrome page-visit import asks for Chrome's history permission, filters records to YouTube video links and lets you choose what to import. These imports do not delete or change your original YouTube or Chrome history.

Website learning records use browser local storage separated by signed-in user and service. Website and extension libraries are separate; signing into Google does not synchronise them across devices. A local backup or PowerPoint you download is a file you control. Treat backups as private: they may include access capabilities for your saved lessons.

What is sent for AI features

When you create a lesson, Delta sends the selected caption excerpt, video identity and title, chosen explanation context, experience level and selected prior learning concepts to its backend and Gemini. When you ask a follow-up, your question and relevant excerpts from the saved lessons you supply are sent for a source-based answer. A source needs readable timestamped captions; a YouTube URL alone does not guarantee usable content.

If you choose AI topic analysis, the selected video metadata is processed to suggest topic groupings. Local history and playback recording do not by themselves authorise uploading your complete browsing history. Delta does not request your Google password, read unrelated websites or sell browsing history for advertising.

Gemini provider processing: this release uses the Gemini Developer API through a Cloud project with active billing. Under the Paid Services section of Google's Gemini API terms, Google does not use prompts or responses to improve its products. Google may retain them for a limited period for abuse prevention, service safety and required legal disclosures. This does not make AI processing local or guarantee that content is never retained or reviewed. Avoid sensitive, confidential or personal information in captions, questions or explanation context. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.

Limited use of user data

Delta uses user data only to provide and support the learning features described in this notice. We do not sell user data, use it for personalised advertising, or use or transfer it for unrelated purposes or creditworthiness or lending decisions. Transfers are limited to providing the service, security, legal obligations or a permitted business transfer. Human access is limited to your specific consent, security or abuse investigations, legal requirements, or aggregated and anonymised internal operations. Delta's use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The provider processing and retention described above still apply.

Google sign-in and free AI allowance

Google sign-in is handled through Firebase Authentication. Delta verifies the resulting identity token on its backend. Firebase processes the Google account identity needed to sign you in; Delta does not receive your password. The extension can open the learning website for sign-in. Website session authentication is separate from extension playback records.

Firestore stores a hashed user identifier, numeric credit totals, hashed session and operation identifiers, operation status and accounting timestamps. The credit ledger does not store your viewing history, captions, questions or lesson text. Credit is tied to your verified account so clearing browser data cannot create another allowance.

The free allowance is up to US$0.50 of estimated AI usage per account for its lifetime, with a separate budget below US$0.10 for each learning session. It is not cash, a recurring balance or a promise of unlimited availability. Unknown failed usage may remain reserved. Shared provider rate limits can temporarily pause requests even when you have credit.

For history-import allowance checks, the website receives the selected YouTube video IDs and a bounded operation identifier. A separate Firestore ledger keeps per-account keyed identifiers, numeric import totals and registration timestamps. It does not keep imported titles, viewing dates or captions. Your account includes ten lifetime distinct-video import registrations. Removing a local copy does not delete the allowance ledger or restore used imports.

Starter Pack payments and purchased-result recovery

The optional one-time US$5 Starter Pack adds 100 distinct-video import registrations and 20 bounded learning sessions when purchases are available. The account screen identifies the payment mode: live Checkout charges US$5 once; Sandbox Checkout is a test and charges no real money. Free estimated AI credit remains separate. The backend verifies the Stripe merchant, Sandbox or live mode, amount, payment state and account ownership before granting allowance. Returning from Checkout does not itself grant anything.

Stripe handles payment details on its hosted checkout page. Delta stores keyed account identifiers, purchase/provider identifiers, payment state, numeric entitlements and capacity/usage accounting. Card numbers and payment credentials are not stored by Delta. Refunds or disputes suspend unused purchased allowance; saved local results remain accessible.

With the operator-approved paid recovery design, a separate Firestore collection stores encrypted paid lesson/source and follow-up result capabilities. These account-bound recovery copies persist beyond seven days, until removed through an account-data request or the service is retired. They support recovery of an identical purchased request after a lost response or server restart. This is not a Google Drive integration or automatic cross-device library synchronisation. Free-result content continues to use the temporary runtime cache described below. Numerical accounting can remain after content deletion to prevent duplicate fulfilment or allowance resets.

Backend processing and retention

The backend runs on Google Cloud Run. It temporarily processes requests and keeps an encrypted private runtime cache of generated lessons and source context, with separate numeric cost receipts. This cache is not a cloud library that synchronises your learning across devices. Cloud Run's runtime storage can disappear when an instance is replaced.

In Google sign-in mode, saved lessons can remain usable after seven days: each request verifies your current Google identity and ownership, and possession of an old access capability alone does not grant access. Anonymous local or guarded-mode capabilities expire after seven days. A downloaded PowerPoint remains your local file. Signing out hides the current website library but does not erase its local records or the lifetime credit ledger. Firebase identity records and the numeric ledger remain to operate authentication and prevent allowance abuse. Hosting and authentication providers may retain operational metadata such as request timing, IP addresses and security logs under their own policies.

Your controls

  • Turn playback recording off without deleting previously saved records.
  • Decline optional history import, revoke Chrome history permission or remove Delta's imported copy through the extension.
  • Export a private local backup or PowerPoint before clearing site or extension data. Clearing that data removes local records and can be irreversible.
  • Sign out to stop using the website under the current Google account. This does not reset used or reserved credit.

For privacy or account-data requests, email the Delta operator at info@vickyai.io. Identify the request and the account involved without sending passwords, API keys, identity tokens, card details or private learning backups. We may need to verify account ownership before acting on a request. Chrome Web Store distribution remains pending. See Help for safe troubleshooting.
